About the Company

SYMBAMAKING THE DIFFERENCE

Systemhaye Mohandessi Barus Oxin (SYMBA) provides artificial lift technologies and integrated engineering services for Iran’s oil and gas industry, specializing in Electric Submersible Pumps (ESP) and Sucker Rod Pumps (SRP). Combining local manufacturing capabilities with international technical expertise, SYMBA supports reliable oil production, equipment localization, and the long-term development of domestic technical capabilities.

Our services cover the equipment lifecycle, from design and supply to installation, operational monitoring, maintenance, failure analysis, and repair including:

  1. 01

    Engineering and Equipment Supply

    Design and supply of ESP systems, surface equipment, Christmas trees, horizontal pumping stations, tubing, and casing.

  2. 02

    Local Manufacturing and Testing

    ESP design, manufacturing, assembly, and testing at SYMBA’s integrated production facility in Mahshahr, Iran, supporting the localization of artificial lift technologies and the development of domestic expertise.

  3. 03

    Installation and Operational Support

    ESP installation, commissioning, and ongoing operational monitoring, supported by telemetry systems to assess equipment performance and support timely technical intervention.

  4. 04

    Equipment Storage and Preservation

    Secure storage and preservation of equipment to maintain its condition and readiness for field deployment.

  5. 05

    Failure Analysis and Repair

    Dismantle, Inspection and Failure Analysis (DIFA), maintenance, and repair at SYMBA’s facility, supporting equipment reliability and helping reduce unplanned downtime.

  6. 06

    Equipment Rental

    Rental of ESP systems and dewatering equipment to meet project and operational requirements.

SYMBA is also the exclusive representative in Iran of an international ESP manufacturer with more than 45 years of experience in artificial lift solutions and annual production exceeding 7,500 ESP units. Through collaboration with established Russian manufacturers, SYMBA combines access to international technology with local engineering, manufacturing, and field support.

This combination positions SYMBA as a technical partner for Iran’s oil industry, focused on sustaining production, extending equipment service life, strengthening local capabilities, and supporting efficient field operations.

OUR PEOPLE, OUR STRENGTH

HR

At Systemhaye Mohandesi Barus Oxin, we regard our people as our most valuable asset and a cornerstone of our success and sustainable organizational development. We believe that our employees’ knowledge, expertise, experience, commitment, creativity, and innovation play a decisive role in enhancing organizational performance and establishing a sustainable competitive advantage in the oil, gas, and energy industries.

By drawing on the capabilities of young, qualified Iranian, Russian, and local professionals and combining current knowledge with the extensive experience of our specialists and managers, we have created an environment that supports knowledge transfer, professional skills development, and the advancement of individual and organizational capabilities in the field of oil pumping systems. This approach enhances productivity and service quality while enabling us to respond effectively to specialized project requirements and developments in the country’s energy industry.

Our strategic approach to human capital extends beyond meeting staffing needs. By aligning our employees’ knowledge and skills with our overarching organizational objectives, strengthening a culture of teamwork, expanding international collaboration, and adopting modern human resource management practices, we are building an agile, knowledge-driven, and competitive organization.

To this end, we continually review our recruitment and hiring policies, develop professional competencies, and draw on the expertise of domestic and international specialists. Through these efforts, we strive to bring together a capable team of managers, engineers, specialists, and operational personnel to deliver projects effectively and achieve the company’s strategic objectives.
