Heavy duty ESP Pump Supply and Commissioning in Iran

SYMBA has achieved a significant milestone in Iran’s oil industry by successfully supplying and commissioning an electric submersible pump (ESP) system with a capacity of 2,000 m³/day and a head of 750 meters in one of the wells of the Cheshmeh Khosh oil field.

EQUIPMENT RELIABILITY

A New Record For 2,437 Days of Continuous ESP Operation

ESP pumps manufactured by SYMBA have achieved a remarkable record of 2,437 days of uninterrupted operation in the West Paydar oil field. This achievement was supported by 24/7 operational monitoring and control, highlighting the importance of continuous performance supervision and effective operational management in enhancing equipment reliability and sustaining oil production and reached to cumulative oil production rate of over 3,000,000 barrels of oil.

ENGINEERING & ENVIRONMENT

Design, Manufacturing, Installation, and Commissioning of an Oil Dewatering Package at Cheshmeh Khosh Field

To solve a major environmental challenge caused by the accumulation of heavy oil in the evaporation lagoon of the Cheshmeh Khosh desalting unit, SYMBA conducted technical studies, drawing on academic research, and designed, manufactured, installed, and commissioned a dedicated oil dewatering and recovery package. This project enabled the extraction and treatment of more than 80,000 barrels of oil from the lagoon to reach to on spec oil, contributing to oil recovery and helping mitigate the environmental challenges associated with the accumulation of heavy oil.

Supply, Service and Maintanence of ESP

Comprehensive ESP support, including technical services, preventive and corrective maintenance, troubleshooting, and logistics. Our specialists provide 24/7 assistance to maximize equipment reliability, minimize downtime, and keep field operations running safely and efficiently.

Skid-Mounted Unit

Location: West Paydar- Ilam Province Capacity: 25,000 barrel per day Project Specification: This project was accomplished for the dehydration of crude oil produced from the Paydar West field, which is a common oil field with Iraq. The engineering, procurement, construction, and commissioning of this unit were accomplished using 100% domestic capabilities from reputable local companies. After the commissioning of this unit, 50% of the produced water from the field has been separated, and the dehydrated crude oil is transported via transfer pumps to the Cheshmeh Khosh Central production facility. Duration of Project construction: 1 year

Manifold Modernization & Separator Repair

Aban High pressure Separator Repair Project: The repair of the Aban manifold separator, along with the upgrading of the process control system in the Aban field manifold, was successfully completed by this company, leading to increased production from this common field with Iraq. In this project, after a thorough inspection and testing of the Aban field separator, an operational repair plan was developed and executed. Following the repairs, the separator was reintegrated into the production line.

Oil Dewatering Of Evaporation Pond(Lagoon)

Dewatering of evaporation pond oil: In this project, utilizing the design and procurement capabilities of fully domestic companies, a unit for separating free water and emulsified water from the oil in the Cheshmeh Khosh desalting unit evaporation pond was designed and implemented. With the execution of this project, the oil in the pond is dehydrated at a production rate and spec aligned with the client's requirements and delivered to the designated point specified by the client.
